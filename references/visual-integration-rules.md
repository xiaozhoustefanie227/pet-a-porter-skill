# Visual Integration Rules

## Preserve the person
Never unnecessarily change:
- face or facial identity
- pose
- body proportions
- skin tone
- original outfit
- key accessories
- bag, shoes, jewelry
- overall camera framing unless needed for pet placement

## Integrate the pet naturally
The pet must match:
- light direction
- perspective
- scale
- depth of field
- ground contact
- cast shadow
- scene color temperature
- overall realism

## Placement guidance
Preferred placement:
- beside the lower leg
- near the feet
- seated slightly in front
- beside furniture or a doorway in interiors
- walking beside the subject in outdoor scenes

## Avoid
- floating pets
- scale that is too large
- impossible hand-holding
- pet pasted in without believable shadow or contact
- pet blocking key outfit elements
