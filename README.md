# Pet-à-Porter 🐾

**Find the pet that matches your style.**

Pet-à-Porter is a fashion-led image skill that turns one outfit photo into a stylish fashion-pet portrait.

Upload a look. The skill reads the person's **Style DNA**, selects a pet that belongs in the same aesthetic world, and adds that pet naturally into the original image.

## What’s new in the final version

This final version strengthens the skill in one key direction:

> **The pet should not only match the person’s vibe — the pet’s styling should also visually echo the person’s outfit.**

That means the skill now matches at **three levels**:

1. **Pet species match** — choose the right animal.
2. **Pet persona match** — choose the right energy and temperament.
3. **Pet styling echo** — lightly reflect the person’s outfit through the pet’s clothing or accessories.

Examples:
- a trench-coat look → dachshund in a tiny trench-style coat;
- a romantic blush dress → Maltese in a soft draped pink outfit;
- a minimalist black look → sleek black cat with a refined collar or no clothing at all;
- a playful Y2K look → Pomeranian with one coordinated accessory.

## Core rule

> **Mirror the aesthetic, then lightly echo the outfit.**

The pet should feel like a stylish companion, not a costumed prop.

## Default modes

- **Perfect Match** — the most natural and stylish companion.
- **Mini Me** — the pet becomes a subtle fashion twin.
- **Fashion Contrast** — an editorial contrast, but still believable.

## Deliverable

The skill outputs:
1. a short editorial-style match line,
2. a final edited image with the pet naturally integrated into the scene.
